Distillation metadata and retained text

Version 2 adds metadata, a complete source partition, annotation buckets and categorized open issues. No text is discarded. Existing points, bindings and gate outcomes are unchanged across 5,000 saved handoffs.

Garbage/noise/social labels may be supplied as exact caller annotations; they are not semantically verified and cannot override the interpretation. Unclassified text remains available for later turns. Evidence coverage does not mean every word was understood.
